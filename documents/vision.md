# JobOS Vision

## Mission

Build the world's smartest AI Career Operating System.

JobOS helps people manage every job application from one place. Instead of opening LinkedIn, Naukri, Wellfound, company career pages and Gmail separately, users get a single AI-powered dashboard that tracks everything.

---

## Problem

Job searching today is broken.

A user applies to jobs across multiple platforms.

Applications become scattered.

Some companies send email updates.

Many companies never send any updates.

Users have to manually log into every portal just to check the latest application status.

People forget follow-ups.

People don't know why they were rejected.

Interview preparation is completely separate.

Resume optimization is manual.

Everything is disconnected.

---

## Solution

JobOS becomes the Career Operating System.

Users connect Gmail and optionally connect supported job platforms.

JobOS automatically creates a job application timeline.

For every application, AI will:

- Track status
- Detect interview invitations
- Detect ghosted applications
- Suggest follow-up reminders
- Match Resume against JD
- Give Resume Match Score
- Explain missing skills
- Suggest resume improvements
- Generate tailored cover letters
- Generate interview questions
- Act as a personal AI Career Assistant

---

## MVP (Version 1)

The first version should focus on solving one problem extremely well.

Core Features:

- Unified Job Dashboard
- Gmail Import
- Manual Job URL Import
- Application Timeline
- Resume Match Score
- JD Analysis
- AI Resume Suggestions
- Interview Preparation
- Follow-up Reminders

---

## Long-Term Vision

JobOS should become the default operating system for every job seeker.

Instead of managing applications manually, users simply open JobOS and AI handles the rest.

Our goal is to become the world's smartest AI-powered career platform.
