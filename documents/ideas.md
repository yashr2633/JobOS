## Unsorted Ideas

- Recruiter-side "Mode" — flip JobOS around so recruiters can see aggregated, anonymized application trends (long-term, may never build).
- Auto-Apply Assistant — AI fills and submits applications on the user's behalf (ethically/legally sensitive, needs real thought before scoping).

- Referral Finder — surface people in the user's network at target companies.
- College/Placement Cell B2B Dashboard — aggregate view for institutions tracking their students' placement progress.
- Mobile App — once web MVP has traction.
